About Us

Thrashing the Crypts (T.T.C.) is a club for metal fans, underground shows, records, shirts, patches, and the culture around heavy music. Fans of all forms of metal are welcome here.

The point is simple: make it easier to find people to go to shows with, organize meetups without making it weird or formal, support bands and venues, and build a local metal community that feels like an actual community.

What fits here?

All forms of metal can be part of the community. Our specialties are death metal, crossover, speed metal, underground thrash, and classic thrash—those are the shows and scenes the club is most likely to follow, without shutting out anyone who comes from another corner of metal.
