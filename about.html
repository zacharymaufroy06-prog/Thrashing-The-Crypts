About Us

Thrashing the Crypts (T.T.C.) is a club for people who are into thrash metal, old-school death metal, underground shows, records, shirts, patches, and the culture around heavy music.

The point is simple: make it easier to find people to go to shows with, organize meetups without making it weird or formal, support bands and venues, and build a local metal community that feels like an actual community.

What fits here?

Classic and underground thrash, death metal, crossover, speed metal, and adjacent heavy stuff. The club can be broad without losing the old-school identity.
